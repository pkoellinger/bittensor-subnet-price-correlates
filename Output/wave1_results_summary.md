# Wave 1 results summary

Generated by `Code/analysis/07_summary.R` on 05 Oct 2026 from the tables in `Output/`. The plan these results follow is `Code/analysis/ANALYSIS-PLAN.md`, frozen in commit `1d034af` before any correlation with the price was computed.

## What the numbers can and cannot say

One cross-section of 128 subnets at the end of September 2026. Features measured at the same time as the price move with it by construction; the only clean time order inside wave 1 is August features against the September price, and the forward test is the wave 2 return (plan, section 9). With 124 regression rows, a standardised coefficient below about 0.2 cannot be told from zero; the cross-validation spread over repeats understates the uncertainty of each R2 (Bates, Hastie and Tibshirani 2023).

## Step 1: descriptives

160 numeric or binary variables and 5 categorical ones (`wave1_descriptives_numeric.csv`, `wave1_descriptives_categorical.csv`). 94 variables have missing values, almost all because the thing measured does not exist for that subnet (no repository: 24 subnets, no X account: 36, no white paper: 96 to 105, no miner paid: 23). Skewness above 2 in 80 variables; counts and amounts are therefore log-transformed in every later step.

## Deviations from the plan

- Amendment 1 (5 Oct 2026, `ANALYSIS-PLAN.md` section 11): a subnet without a GitHub repository or an X account gets the weakest observed value on every repository or X variable instead of the average of the subnets that have one. The OLS results are unchanged by construction (the indicators `has_repo` and `has_x` absorb any constant fill); the penalised models and the random forest were rerun.
- `miner_paid` and `started` (and `has_holders` in specification 3) are exactly collinear with the flags `flag_full_burn_30d` and `startup_mode` in the 124-row sample and were dropped by the fitting routine; the fit tables note it per model.
- `flag_placeholder_identity` and `startup_mode` were moved from prediction set F2 to F1 after the pre-analysis commit, so that F1 carries the controls of specification 1 as the plan states.
- `startup_mode` was taken out of specification 1 (and of the rehearsal model): `started_lag` nests it, and the one subnet that started emissions in September but not in August (netuid 36) would otherwise be the only observation identifying it, with leverage 1 and undefined HC3 standard errors.
- Specification 1 includes the lagged attention composite (August commentator posts and podcasts), which specification 2 does not include at all (attention is a two-way feature and enters in specification 3). The two R2 values are therefore not a like-for-like comparison of August and September state.
